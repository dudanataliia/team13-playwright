# TC-10 — Author can edit their own news and changes are saved

**Description:**
Verify that the author can edit their own news and the changes are saved.

**Preconditions:**

- Author is logged in.
- News post exists.

**Steps:**

- Open the news post.
- Click the "Edit news" button.
- Modify title, content, and tags.
- Click "Submit".
- Reload or open the news post.

**Expected Result:**

- News is updated with the new values.
- Original creation date remains unchanged.

Automated Verification:

- Assert that title/content/tags are updated.
- Assert that the "created date" has not changed.

---

## Automation notes

- The test creates a fresh news post (precondition), opens it, captures the original
  creation date, then edits it and verifies the saved result.
- Clicking "Edit news" (`.edit-news`) opens the create-news form in edit mode at
  `#/greenCity/news/create-news?id={id}` with the title, content and tags pre-filled.
- The functional save control in edit mode is the bottom green primary button
  (`button.primary-global-button:not(.s-btn)`, labelled "Edit"). It is reused via
  `createNewsPage.submitEdit()`, which waits for the redirect away from the form.
- The creation date is read from `.news-info-date`, content from `.news-text-content`,
  title from `.news-title`, and tags from `.tags-item` (compared case-insensitively).

### Note: "Submit" vs "Edit" button labelling

- The sticky header shows a button labelled **"Submit"** (`primary-global-button.s-btn`),
  but clicking it does **not** save the form. The actual save button is the bottom green
  button labelled **"Edit"**. The test uses the working button; the spec's "Submit" wording
  corresponds to this control.

## Result: PASS

- Title, content and tags are updated and persisted after editing.
- The original creation date remains unchanged.

> Note: the image cropper exposes the **"Submit"** (`primary-global-button.s-btn`) button,
> which applies the image crop. The whole edited form is saved with the bottom green
> **"Edit"** button (the sticky-header "Submit" does not save the form).
