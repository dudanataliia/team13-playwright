# TC-07 — Cancel confirmation modal

**Description:** Verify that clicking the "Cancel" button triggers a confirmation modal and that selecting "Yes, cancel" closes the form and returns the user to the news page.

**Preconditions:**

- The user is logged into the system.
- The "Create News" form is open.
- The form fields are filled (e.g., "Title" = "Test", "Main Text" = "Test content with 20 chars").

**Steps:**

1. Navigate to [GreenCity News](https://www.greencity.cx.ua/#/greenCity/news) and click "Create News".
2. Enter the following values:

- Title: "Test"
- Main Text: "Test content with 20 chars"
- Click the "Cancel" button.

3. Verify that a confirmation modal appears with the message:

- "All created content will be lost. Do you still want to cancel news creating?"
- Click "Yes, cancel".

4. Verify that:

- The form closes.
- The user is redirected to the news page.

5. Repeat steps 1-4, but this time click "Continue editing".
6. Verify that:

- The modal closes.
- The user remains on the "Create News" form with all previously entered data intact.

**Expected Result:**

- Clicking "Cancel" triggers a confirmation modal with the correct message.
- Clicking "Yes, cancel" closes the form and redirects the user to the news page.
- Clicking "Continue editing" dismisses the modal and keeps the form open with existing data.

**Automated Verification:**

- Check the text and appearance of the confirmation modal.
- Verify that clicking "Yes, cancel" closes the form and redirects to the news page.
- Ensure that clicking "Continue editing" dismisses the modal and keeps the form unchanged.

---

## Automation notes

- The confirmation modal is a Material dialog (`mat-dialog-container`) modelled by the
  `ConfirmModal` component with `Yes, cancel` (`.primary-global-button`),
  `Continue editing` (`.secondary-global-button`) and a close (X) button.
- The modal message matches the spec exactly:
  `All created content will be lost. Do you still want to cancel news creating?`

### Result: PASS

- "Cancel" opens the modal with the correct message and both buttons.
- "Yes, cancel" closes the form and redirects to `#/greenCity/news`.
- "Continue editing" dismisses the modal and keeps the form open with the Title and Main Text intact.
