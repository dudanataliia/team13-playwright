# TC-05 — Main Text validation (min 20, max 63,206 chars)

**Description:** Verify the validation of the "Main Text" field (min. 20, max. 63,206 characters) and ensure that the "Publish" button remains disabled if the text is too short.

**Preconditions:**

- The user is logged into the system.
- The "Create News" form is open.

**Steps:**

1. Navigate to [GreenCity News](https://www.greencity.cx.ua/#/greenCity/news) and click "Create News".
2. Enter 10 characters in the "Main Text" field (e.g., "Short text").
3. Fill in the "Title" field with "Test".
4. Click "Publish".
5. Verify that:
- An error message appears in red:
- "Must be a minimum of 20 and a maximum of 63,206 symbols."
- The "Publish" button remains disabled.

6. Enter 63,207 characters in the "Main Text" field.
7. Verify that:
- The text is truncated to 63,206 characters.
- No error message appears.

8. Enter 25 characters in the "Main Text" field (e.g., "This is a valid test content").
9. Verify that:
- The error message disappears.
- The "Publish" button becomes enabled.
- Click "Publish".
- Verify that the news is published successfully without errors.

**Expected Result:**

- If less than 20 characters are entered:
- The error message appears in red.
- The "Publish" button remains disabled.
- If more than 63,206 characters are entered, the text is truncated.
- Text between 20 and 63,206 characters is accepted, and the "Publish" button becomes enabled.

**Automated Verification:**

- Check the presence and color of the warning message when text is too short.
- Verify that the "Publish" button is disabled until the text length is valid.
- Ensure that text does not exceed 63,206 characters.
- Confirm that news is successfully published when the text is valid.

---

## Automation notes

- "Main Text" is a Quill rich-text editor (`.ql-editor`, contenteditable), not a plain input.
- A valid Title and a selected tag are also required for "Publish" to enable, so the test fixes
  both and varies only the Main Text length to isolate this validation.
- The validation message lives in `.field-info`; it carries the `warning` class (red,
  `rgb(235, 24, 13)`) for invalid text and loses it (grey) for valid text.

### Functional behaviour confirmed (passes)

- 10 characters → red warning shown, "Publish" disabled.
- 25 characters → warning cleared, "Publish" enabled, news published successfully.

### Detected product bugs (test fails by design)

1. **Wrong warning wording**

   | Expected (spec) | Actual (UI) |
   | --- | --- |
   | `Must be a minimum of 20 and a maximum of 63,206 symbols.` | `Must be minimum 20 and maximum 63 206 symbols` |

   Missing "a … of", `63 206` instead of `63,206`, and no trailing period.

2. **No truncation on over-limit input** — entering 63,207 characters is **not** truncated to
   63,206 (the field keeps 63,207) and an **error is shown**, whereas the spec requires silent
   truncation to 63,206 with no error.
